## Effective Date: 2026-09-19 | Version V1.0

We ("HealthTracker") take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the HealthTracker mobile application (the "App"). **By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.**

### 1. Information We Collect

The App is a local-first health tracking tool. **Your health data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any of our servers.**

| Data Type | Details | Storage |
|---|---|---|
| Blood sugar records | Values, measurement period (fasting/post-meal), timestamps | Local only |
| Blood pressure records | Systolic, diastolic, pulse, timestamps | Local only |
| Weight / BMI | Weight values, BMI (auto-calculated), timestamps | Local only |
| Heart rate records | Resting heart rate, category, timestamps | Local only |
| Water intake records | Volume per drink, timestamps | Local only |
| Pedometer data | Steps, distance, calories (from device sensors) | Local only |
| Basic profile | Age, gender, height (used solely for BMI calculation and result assessment) | Local only |
| Reminder settings | Custom water/measurement alarm times | Local only |

### 2. Use of Device Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Fingertip heart-rate measurement | Used only to analyze fingertip blood-flow color changes in real time; **no photos or videos are taken, and no images are saved**. Processing is entirely on-device |
| Activity Recognition | Pedometer | Reads the system step-counter sensor to display daily steps |
| Notifications | Health reminders | Sends reminders you have set up yourself |
| Receive boot completed | Restore alarms | Restores your reminders after device restart |
| Foreground service | Persistent reminders | Ensures your enabled reminders fire on time |

All permissions are requested when the corresponding feature is first used and may be declined; declining only affects that feature.

### 3. Third-Party Services

| Service | Provider | Purpose | Data Processed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firebase Analytics | Google LLC | Anonymous usage statistics, product improvement | Anonymous device identifiers, event data (never your health data) |
| Google AdMob | Google LLC | Showing ads to keep the App free | Advertising identifier, device info, coarse location |
| Google Play In-App Review | Google LLC | Inviting you to rate the App | No additional personal information |

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or other applicable regions, the App requests consent for personalized advertising via a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (UMP) at first launch. You may withdraw this consent at any time in system settings.

**Your health records are never transmitted through any of these services.**

### 4. How Data Is Stored and Protected
- Health data is stored locally; no cloud sync, no servers holding your data
- No account system; no name, email, phone number, or directly identifying information is collected
- We recommend setting a device screen lock to protect local data

### 5. Data Export and Deletion
- **Export**: Settings → Export Data (CSV, via the system share menu)
- **Deletion**: Delete individual records in each module's history; uninstalling the App **permanently deletes** all data on the device

### 6. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Except for the third-party services described in Section 3, we do not share data with anyone else unless required by law.

### 7. Children's Privacy
The App is intended for users aged 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction) or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 8. AI Health Q&A Disclaimer
The in-app "AI Health Assistant" provides general health knowledge for reference only. It does not constitute medical advice and cannot replace diagnosis by a licensed physician. **For any health concerns, please consult a qualified doctor.**

### 9. Changes to This Policy
Material changes will be highlighted in the App or on this page. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
