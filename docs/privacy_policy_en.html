HealthTracker Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026  |  Version: V1.0

We ("HealthTracker") take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the HealthTracker mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App is a local-first health tracking tool. Your health data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any of our servers. This includes:

Data TypeDetailsStorage
Blood sugar recordsValues, measurement period (fasting/post-meal), timestampsLocal only
Blood pressure recordsSystolic, diastolic, pulse, timestampsLocal only
Weight / BMIWeight values, BMI (auto-calculated), timestampsLocal only
Heart rate recordsResting heart rate, category, timestampsLocal only
Water intake recordsVolume per drink, timestampsLocal only
Pedometer dataSteps, distance, calories (from device sensors)Local only
Basic profileAge, gender, height (used solely for BMI calculation and result assessment)Local only
Reminder settingsCustom water/measurement alarm timesLocal only

Your health records (blood sugar, blood pressure, heart rate, weight, water, steps) are never transmitted through any of these services. Ad and analytics SDKs only access device-level identifiers.

4. How Data Is Stored and Protected

  • Your health data is stored locally on your device. The App offers no cloud sync and operates no servers holding your data.
  • The App requires no account registration and collects no name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.
  • We recommend setting a device screen lock to protect your local data.

5. Data Export and Deletion

  • Export: You can export all health records as a CSV file via "Settings → Export Data" and save or transfer it yourself via the system share menu.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual records in each module's history; uninstalling the App permanently deletes all data stored on the device, with no way to recover it.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Except for the third-party services described in Section 3, we do not share data with anyone else unless required by law or necessary to protect our legitimate rights.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction) or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Health data is entered voluntarily by the user and stored only locally.

8. AI Health Q&A Disclaimer

The in-app "AI Health Assistant" provides general health knowledge for reference only. It does not constitute medical advice and cannot replace diagnosis by a licensed physician. Its responses are not generated from your health records. For any health concerns, please consult a qualified doctor.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be highlighted in the App or on this page. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
